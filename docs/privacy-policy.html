Privacy Policy — Anviksha

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters. In short: Anviksha keeps everything on your device and sends nothing about you anywhere. The app is offline-first and requires no account.

1. No data collection

Anviksha does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. The app works fully offline.

2. Everything stays on your device

Your learning progress, achievements, and settings are saved only in local storage on your phone. This data is never uploaded to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

3. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local reminders (a daily challenge and a lesson nudge) directly on your device. These are generated on-device — no push servers, and no messages are sent to us. You can turn them off anytime in the app's Settings or your system settings.

4. Device features

The app may use vibration for haptic feedback and play in-app sounds. Both are optional and controlled in Settings. Neither reads nor shares any personal data.

5. Links that leave the app

Some actions open external websites in your browser — learning resources, a contact form, rating on the Play Store, sharing, and voluntary donations (PayPal / UPI). Once you leave the app, those third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive any information back from them.

6. Children

Anviksha is a general-audience educational app suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

7. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to breach on our side. All app data lives only on your own device.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions? Reach out through the in-app contact form under the app menu.
